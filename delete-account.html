Delete Your IdrissAgent Account

At IdrissAgent (developed by Abdellatif Benbaha), we respect your privacy and give you full control over your data.

How to delete your account:
To request the deletion of your account and all associated data, please send an email to:

Email: abdellatif.benbaha87@gmail.com
Subject: Delete My Account
Include: The email address you used to register.

What data will be deleted?

Once we receive your request, we will delete the following data within 7 days:

  • Your account credentials (Email, Password).
  • Your Wallet balance and transaction history.
  • Your active subscription status and token reservations.
  • Your feature usage counters and tool call sessions.
  • Your local chat history and user profiles (this data is stored on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app).

What data is kept and why?

We retain anonymized cost tracking logs (such as AI API costs) for financial analytics and to prevent abuse. These logs do not contain your email, password, or chat content. They are linked only to a random UUID that cannot identify you after account deletion.

Third-Party AI Providers

When you use AI features, your input is sent to third-party providers. Their retention policies:

  • DeepSeek: Data may be retained according to their privacy policy (not publicly specified).
  • Google Gemini: Paid API logs retained for up to 55 days, not used for training.
  • Replicate: API predictions auto-deleted after 1 hour.
  • Cloudflare: Worker logs retained for 3 days (Free plan).
⚠️ Note: We cannot control third-party providers' retention. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for more information.

Data Retention Timeline

All personal data is permanently deleted from our servers (Supabase) within 7 days of your request. Anonymized financial logs are kept indefinitely for accounting purposes.
